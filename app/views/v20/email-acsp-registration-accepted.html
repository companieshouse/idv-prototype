Register as an ACSP: application approved

We have approved your application and registered MORRIS ACCOUNTING LTD as an Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP). We’ve created an agent account for the ACSP.

Sign in to the agent account

Sign in using your GOV.UK One Login to access the account for MORRIS ACCOUNTING LTD:

https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sign-in-to-your-authorised-agent-account

Save this link to access the account in future.

What to do next

  1. Add other people who work for the business to the account.
    They’ll be able to log in using their own GOV.UK One Login to use services for the ACSP.
  2. Save the link to our guidance and services for ACSPs.
    You can use it to access everything you need as an ACSP.
  3. Make sure the ACSP details are correct and up to date.
    You, or another account owner, must tell us about any changes to the ACSP details within 14 days. You can view and update the details form the account dashboard.
