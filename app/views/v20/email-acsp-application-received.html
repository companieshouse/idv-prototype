Register as a Companies House authorised agent: application received

Business name MORRIS ACCOUNTING LTD
Company number 02050399
Application reference HDJ2123F

We’ll send you an email to let you know if your application has been accepted or rejected.

What happens if the application is accepted

We’ll register MORRIS ACCOUNTING LTD as an Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP).

We’ll create an agent account for the ACSP, which you’ll be able to sign in to using your GOV.UK One Login.

We recommend saving a link to our guidance and services for ACSPs. You can use it to access everything you need as an ACSP, including to sign in to the agent account when it’s ready.

What happens if the application is rejected

We'll explain why it's been rejected and what you can do next. We'll also refund the £63 registration fee.
