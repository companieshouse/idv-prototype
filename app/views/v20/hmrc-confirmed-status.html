You need to be approved by HMRC before you can register

You cannot register as a Companies House authorised agent until HMRC has approved your application for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) supervision.

Check the HMRC Supervised Business Register (opens in a new tab) to check your supervision status.

Save and sign out

You can save your application and sign out while you wait for HMRC to approve your application.

Once HMRC has approved your application, sign in and go to Your filings to continue your application.
