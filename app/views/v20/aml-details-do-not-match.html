Check your AML name and registration number

What you need to check

You need to check:

  • the exact name you are registered with
  • your AML registration number

HMRC

If HMRC is your supervisory body, you need to check that your status is ‘approved’ on the HMRC Supervised Business Register (opens in a new tab).

How to check

You can:

  • sign in to your supervisory body account
  • check your supervisory body's public register
  • contact your supervisory body

For more guidance on How to check your Anti-Money Laundering (AML) details (opens in a new tab).

Save and sign out

You can save and sign out of this service. Once you have checked your AML details, you can return to the service and continue your application.
