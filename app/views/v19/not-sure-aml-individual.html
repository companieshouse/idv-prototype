Check your AML details

What you need to check

You need to check:

  • the exact name you are registered with
  • the number you are registered with for AML

HMRC

If you are supervised with HMRC, you also need to check that you're status is 'approved' on the registered list (opens in a new tab).

How to check

You can:

  • sign in to your supervisory body account
  • Contact your supervisory body
  • Check the supervisory bodies public list

For more guidance on How to check your Anti-Money Laundering (AML) details (opens in a new tab).

Sign out and continue with registering
